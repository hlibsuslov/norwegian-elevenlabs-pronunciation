# Lessons from the first QA recording

The balanced scan returned seven short spectral transients. Relaxing existing thresholds increased the queue to 27 while its one match with a rough artifact point stayed the same. Keep that preset as an option and assess its cost on independent recordings.

The experimental local spectral-change score can increase approximate point coverage at the price of a larger review queue. At 6.5 it placed 14 of the 17 rough artifact marks near candidates and exposed over 17 minutes of review intervals. The 17 anchor windows and 56 nearby unlabeled comparison windows yield an exploratory rank AUC of about 0.666. This measures separation of approximate windows in one recording.

For mixed-language instruction, identify the word, its expected locale and the language of the explanatory context independently. The two targeted ASR passes placed the candidate `holder` earlier than the reported point. The phone model scored an American English sequence higher than the Norwegian hypotheses on the same three crops. Store the dictionary contrast and route this audio to native review before adding a production correction.

Keep four update types explicit. Rule calibration changes thresholds or ranking. Example updates add reviewed audio and model-versioned vectors. Prompt iterations change the local audio review question. Classifier training requires independent recording, voice and model splits and a frozen evaluation procedure. The first cycle exercised the first three.

Reviewers record precise boundaries and preserve uncertain cases. Candidate retrieval excludes the source digest and checks embedding model identity, so later recordings can use reviewed examples without learning from themselves.
