# Tonem 1 and Tonem 2

In the Eastern NB Uttale profile, `bønder` and `bønner` form a matched segmental example with different lexical tone labels. The skill verifies the distinction using the official NoFAbet and IPA columns. The synthesized segmental IPA can be identical for both words. Keep the lexical label separately and audition pitch realization in each context.

Lexical tone is realized on a contour conditioned by dialect, sentence focus, the following syllables and phrasing. Some dialects make different distinctions or use different contours. Compare time-normalized and speaker-relative pitch together with duration and intensity on context-matched speech. Require native evidence to name a realized toneme or confirm a tonal error. The implemented reference comparison flags acoustic deviations and reports lexical labels. It does not classify heard toneme from raw F0 or from successful recognition.
