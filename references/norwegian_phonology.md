# Norwegian phonology and sources

Use NB Uttale v1.0 `e_written` for Bokmål read speech in the Eastern profile. NB Uttale also offers Eastern, Western, Southwestern, Northern and Central written and spoken profiles. Each describes a broad regional model. Match the selected voice and target recording to the desired locality where practical.

NB Uttale fields include wordform, POS, morphology, wordform ID, update source, NoFAbet, source IPA and X-SAMPA. Preserve all three transcriptions for review. NoFAbet specifies nucleus stress and tone codes. The source IPA encodes tone 1 and tone 2 with ASCII markers. The skill maps both markers to primary stress for engine-facing IPA while keeping the original markers and codes in the report. Length marks and explicitly listed retroflex consonants come directly from the dictionary. Treat consonant duration in audio as a separate observation. Between words, retroflex assimilation, reduction and rhythm depend on the utterance and voice.

Check proper names and places in context, including the bearer or local source when relevant. Candidate syllabification from a general dictionary can differ in spontaneous speech. For numerals, dates, currency, acronyms and foreign spellings, preserve the writing and audition the selected model's spoken normalization under the actual context. Numeral forms may need an exact, reviewed lexical expansion. Flag unknown pronunciations and retain uncertainty.
