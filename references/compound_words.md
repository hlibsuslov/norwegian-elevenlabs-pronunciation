# Compounds and long words

Prefer the full, inflected NB Uttale entry and retain multiple transcriptions when it offers them. Review the placement of hovedtrykk and bitrykk with the NoFAbet markers. For a missing long word, the analyzer may list two-part decompositions across a possible linking `s`, `e` or hyphen. Treat these splits as search hints for a human review. A whole word can differ from concatenated constituent pronunciations because of assimilation, syllabification, stress and local speech practice.

Keep full compounds intact in the manuscript and inside naturally phrased synthesis chunks. When a correction needs experimental IPA, use a source-attested full-word target or a reviewed correction record. Treat pronunciation of a new compound and meaning of a lexical segmentation as distinct evidence.
